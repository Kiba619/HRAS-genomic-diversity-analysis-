# Why diversity in genomic data matters: A look at the *HRAS* variants 

## Overview
A clinical genomic data analysis looking at the benign/likely benign and pathogenic variants of *HRAS* and the differences in population distribution in genetic ancestry groups. 

## Research question:
How do allele frequencies of *HRAS* variants differ across genetic ancestry groups? 

## Data sources and tools 
* **CliniVar**: Used to find *HRAS* variant 
* **gnomAD**: Used to find the 4 variants of *HRAS* and the HGVS consequence (`p.Gly60Asp`, `p.Ser89Cys`, `p.Arg164Arg`, `c.*43C>T`) 
* **Excel**: Used to store raw data of genetic ancestry groups and graphs to represent findings
* **Canva**: Used to present infographic
